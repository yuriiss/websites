Simulators

Train by doing — 5 interactive simulators

IT / OT Attack Vectors

CS Attack Simulator

Launch phishing, infected-USB and GPS-spoofing attacks against a virtual vessel and watch them propagate across IT and OT zones — and learn why segmentation is the last line of defence.

Bridge Team Resilience

NAVI Cyber Attack Simulator

Con a vessel through the Dover Strait on a live ECDIS/radar overlay while GNSS creeping spoofs, ECDIS kernel attacks and alarm flooding hit the bridge. Initiate manual fallback and keep her safe.

OT Isolation Drill

OT Cyber Attack Simulator

An attacker executes remote commands on the ballast water management system. Acknowledge, switch to LOCAL/MANUAL, cut the WAN uplink — in the right order, under pressure.

Human-Factor Drill

Phishing Simulator

A convincing "Port State Control" email threatens fines and detention. Find every red flag before the attachment runs — the exact pattern that compromises real vessels.

IMO & IACS Compliant Audit

SentinelGuard Simulator

Run drills, manage incidents, maintain the asset inventory and complete an IMO 2021 / IACS UR E26-E27 audit checklist for MV Sentinel-Alpha — with a live security-posture score.

Frameworks

Aligned with industry frameworks

  • IMO Resolution MSC.428(98) — cyber risk management in the SMS
  • ISM Code (SOLAS Chapter IX) — documented procedures and audits
  • IACS UR E26 & E27 — cyber resilience of ships and onboard systems
  • USCG Final Rule 33 CFR Part 101 Subpart F — training, drills, CySO, incident reporting
  • NIST Cybersecurity Framework & IMO MSC-FAL.1/Circ.3
  • Supports a company's wider cyber training programme
