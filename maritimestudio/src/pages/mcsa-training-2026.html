Course Architecture

One foundation. Three professional tracks.

Every participant completes a shared 8-hour Core Foundation, then branches into the track built for their role. Depth, exercises and assessment differ by track — the framework mapping stays consistent across all three.

8hCORE

Core Foundation · 8 hours

The maritime cyber threat landscape · IMO cyber risk management framework — Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover · NIST CSF 2.0 for maritime · cyber risk fundamentals and the CIA methodology.

Track A · Seafarers

Deck & engine officers, ratings, masters

16 hours
  • Shipboard cyber awareness — phishing, social engineering, USB threats
  • OT/IT systems & network segmentation onboard
  • ECDIS, AIS & GPS vulnerabilities; GNSS spoofing recognition
  • Incident detection and first response at sea
  • Secure remote access & third-party technician protocols
  • Contingency planning and manual operation fallbacks
Track B · Shipbuilders

Shipyard engineers, designers, system integrators

20 hours
  • IACS UR E26/E27 and secure-by-design principles
  • Security zones and conduit design
  • Cybersecurity Design Description (CSDD) documentation
  • Supplier security requirements & UR E27 compliance topics
  • Commissioning, testing & handover cyber validation
  • Supply chain security and vendor risk management
Track C · Office Staff

CSO, DPA, IT/OT managers, shore personnel

24 hours
  • Cybersecurity Plan (CSP) development and governance
  • Cyber risk assessment and CIA methodology
  • USCG 33 CFR 101 and incident reporting
  • EU NIS2 compliance and management body liability
  • ISO 27001 / NIST CSF 2.0 implementation
  • Cyber exercises, drills and tabletop scenarios
